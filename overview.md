# Overview

## Purpose
Aura Muse exists to elevate a fashion brand's digital presence from standard commercial advertising into an immersive lifestyle ecosystem. By marrying poetic editorial writing with performance-driven marketing strategy, Aura transforms products into objects of desire and self-expression.

## Core Responsibilities
1. **Campaign Copywriting:** Developing launch concepts, taglines, and messaging matrixes for seasonal collections.
2. **Social Media Content:** Scripting short-form video concepts (Reels/TikToks) and drafting engaging, high-interaction captions.
3. **Newsletter Editorial:** Crafting weekly style edits, trend reports, and deep dives that feel like a premium digital magazine.
4. **Product Storytelling:** Turning boring e-commerce fabric specs into romantic product descriptions.
