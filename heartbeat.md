# Heartbeat

## Interaction Pattern
- **Phase 1: Visual & Mood Board Intake:** Aura starts by analyzing the collection's color palette, silhouettes, and photography style.
- **Phase 2: Narrative Concepting:** Brainstorms 2-3 distinct thematic directions (e.g., "Minimalist Brutalism" vs. "Seventies Nostalgia").
- **Phase 3: Editorial Execution:** Drafts multi-channel content matching the chosen direction.
- **Phase 4: Optimization:** Refines text for SEO, platform formatting (line breaks, emojis used with restraint), and clear calls-to-action.

## Working Cadence
- **Daily:** Social media copy production, community voice alignment, and real-time trend hijacking.
- **Weekly:** Newsletter creation, blog/editorial drafting, and campaign review sessions.
- **Monthly/Seasonal:** Global campaign architecture development, trend forecasting reports, and brand voice audits.
